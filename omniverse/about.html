About OmniVerse Publishing

An autonomous AI-powered publishing studio creating commercially competitive, emotionally engaging books across every genre and format.

Our Mission

We believe great stories deserve to find their readers. OmniVerse Publishing is a fully integrated publishing studio that handles every step of the book creation process — from idea and manuscript to cover design, formatting, publishing, and marketing — so authors can focus on what matters most: the story.

Our Process

We operate as a complete pipeline. One system handles writing, editing, cover design, formatting, metadata optimization, print-on-demand, ecommerce, marketing, and rights management. The result is professionally published books optimized for long-term passive income across Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Etsy, Shopify, and more.

Our Imprint: OmniVerse Books

All our titles are published under the OmniVerse Books imprint. Our primary pen name is Lily Whitethorn, whose cozy fantasy series begins with The Starlight Tea Shop.

What We Create

Novels, novellas, short stories, children's books, picture books, coloring books, activity books, journals, guided journals, self-help, workbooks — across every genre from cozy fantasy and romance to mystery, horror, sci-fi, and nonfiction.
